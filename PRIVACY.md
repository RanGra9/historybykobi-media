# HistoryByKobi – Privacy Policy

HistoryByKobi is a non-commercial memorial project that publishes short history stories from the archive of Kobi Granot to its own Instagram account (@historybykobi) and its own YouTube channel.

- The app is used only by the project owner to upload videos to the project's own YouTube channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users.
- Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are used solely to upload and manage the channel's own videos.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: through the Instagram account @historybykobi.

Last updated: October 2026.
